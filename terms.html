1. What the service is

GTA6 Clips DE ("the app") is a personal tool operated by the owner of the GTA6 Clips DE accounts. It creates short highlight clips from streams and videos and posts them to the owner's own social media accounts.

2. Who can use it

The app is not offered to the public. Only the account owner signs in with TikTok and authorizes the app to post on their own TikTok account. No other users can register.

3. Content

Clips are only made from creators who have given permission to clip their content. Each clip credits the original creator. Each clip is reviewed by hand before it is posted. Rights holders can request removal at any time (see "Contact").

Use of TikTok through the app is also subject to TikTok's own Terms of Service. The app only uses the permissions the account owner grants (user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload) and can be disconnected at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
