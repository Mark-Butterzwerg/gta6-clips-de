Privacy Policy

GTA6 Clips DE · Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Summary

GTA6 Clips DE is a personal tool that runs on the operator's own computer. It does not collect data from the public, has no user accounts, no tracking, no cookies and no advertising.

2. TikTok data

When the account owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token, a refresh token and the account's open ID from TikTok. These are stored only locally on the operator's computer and are used solely to post approved clips to that same account. They are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.

3. Video content

Stream recordings and clips are processed locally. To write short captions, a short text transcript of a clip candidate may be sent to an AI service provider (Anthropic) for analysis. No TikTok account data is included.

4. Storage and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. Disconnecting the app in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) revokes access; local tokens are then deleted.

5. Viewers of posted clips

The app does not collect any data about people who watch the posted clips. Viewing happens on TikTok and is governed by TikTok's privacy policy.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or removal requests, please open an issue in this project's GitHub repository.
